Access keys (fh_usr_)

Make an fh_usr_ access key for a script or server of yours, scoped to the projects and actions it needs. How long keys last, when FilesHub shows one again, rotating with a 24-hour overlap, and revoking.

Connect an AI assistant (MCP at /mcp/account)

Add FilesHub to Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code or Cursor with one address, https://fileshub.zaions.com/mcp/account. You approve each assistant on FilesHub, pick its projects and what it may do, and every delete it asks for waits for your yes.

Other apps — your FilesHub projects in your own tools

People who belong to FilesHub projects can connect their own scripts with fh_usr_ access keys, AI assistants over MCP, and servers with signed webhooks. What each door reaches, what it never reaches, and who switches it on.

The person API (/api/account/v1)

Two-step sign-in for people
