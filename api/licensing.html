Licensing

Our products can be bought as a white-label licence (we run the product under the buyer's name, on their domain) or as a source buyout (the buyer receives the code, and the product is no longer sold). FilesHub holds the catalogue, computes every price, opens the checkout, issues the licence keys and answers every verification, so no product keeps a licence backend of its own.

Availability

Added in backend 2026.11.13.1; check GET https://fileshub.zaions.com/api/version.

Base URL https://fileshub.zaions.com/api/licensing/v1. No API key: product pages, docs sites and buyers' own hosts call these routes, and CORS is open. A product is named by its FilesHub slug. Every route is throttled per IP.

Responses use one envelope: success {"data": …}; failure {"error": "machine_code", "message": "…"} (validation failures add errors).

Catalogue and price​

RouteWhat it answers
GET /products/{slug}/catalogThe groups on offer with their prices, the bundle discount, the buyout, the update plans, sales_state and checkout_available
POST /products/{slug}/quoteThe total for {"kind": "white_label", "groups": [...]}, {"kind": "buyout"} or {"kind": "renewal", "months": 6}

sales_state is on_sale (online checkout), quote_only (send a request) or acquired (bought out; no new licences). A product's pages read the catalogue at build time and again on load, and never compute a total themselves.

curl -s -X POST https://fileshub.zaions.com/api/licensing/v1/products/clearhire/quote \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"kind":"white_label","groups":["web","mobile"]}'
# {"data":{"kind":"white_label","groups":["web","mobile"],"subtotal_usd":20000,"discount_pct":10,
#          "discount_usd":2000,"total_usd":18000,"updates_months":6,"months":null}}

Buying​

RouteBody
POST /products/{slug}/checkoutkind (white_label or buyout), groups, buyer country, notes, turnstile_token, success_path, cancel_path
POST /products/{slug}/renewalspublic_key, email (the one on the licence), months, turnstile_token
POST /products/{slug}/inquirieskind (quote, custom_work, buyout_call), name, email, company, groups, message, turnstile_token
GET /orders/{order_id}One read on the thank-you page: status (awaiting_payment, payment_submitted, payment_rejected or paid) and, once paid, the licence key

A licence is paid by bank transfer to our Wise account, never through a card checkout. The checkout opens an order at the total FilesHub computed and answers {"data": {"checkout_url": "…", "order_id": "…"}}; the page sends the buyer to that URL in the same tab. It is FilesHub's own pay page, which shows the order, the account details and a form for the transfer reference, amount, date and an optional receipt. Paths are joined to the product's own site. Nothing is granted by the redirect or by sending the details: we match the transfer against our account, and only then is the licence issued and the certificate emailed. If no payment method is published, checkout_available is false and pages offer the request form instead.

turnstile_token is required when the product has a Turnstile secret in its FilesHub vault.

Verifying a licence​

RouteAnswers
GET /licenses/verify?key=KEYverdict: valid, pending_setup, revoked, not_found or invalid_format, with the public fields of the licence
GET /licenses/verify?key=KEY&host=HOSTThe same, plus host_authorized — the check a white-label build runs once on start
GET /licenses/verify?domain=HOST&product=SLUGFilesHub reads https://HOST/.well-known/{prefix}-white-label-license.json and checks the key it names: authorized, domain_mismatch, revoked, not_a_valid_license, no_file or unreachable
GET /licenses/{key}/certificate.pdfThe public certificate

Verification answers never carry an email, a price or the private key. A licence covers each recorded domain and its subdomains.

Management API​

Agents set a product's offer with an fh_pat_ token: GET|PUT /api/public/v1/projects/{project}/license-offer (writes need Can Write Vault), GET /api/public/v1/projects/{project}/licenses, and POST /api/public/v1/projects/{project}/licenses/import for licences issued before FilesHub held them (idempotent on public_key). See the OpenAPI document.
