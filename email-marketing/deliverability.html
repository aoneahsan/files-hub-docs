Mailbox warm-up and deliverability

Since backend 2026.10.20.1. FilesHub sends from your own mailboxes, so their reputation is yours to keep. Three server-side parts do that work, and the Management API exposes their numbers and controls.

Warm-up, for every mailbox​

Any mailbox with Warm-up switched on in Nova takes part, whatever its purpose. Between 08:00 and 20:00 a mailbox sends at most one short plain-text note every 10 minutes, spread across the day. Each purpose has its own ramp:

PurposeNotes per day (first day → step → most)Share of threads answered
Marketing3 → +2 → 4065%
Outreach2 → +1 → 2050%
Transactional2 → +1 → 1550%
Warm-up partnerreplies only, up to 1545%

A warm-up partner is an extra inbox (for example a long-standing Gmail account). It only receives, reads and answers warm-up notes. It never starts a thread and never sends campaign or transactional mail.

Each mailbox writes as a persona to a few regular correspondents. Every pair shares a story that moves slowly through life chapters, and a thread goes back and forth 2–5 times using its last notes as memory. Notes are written by AI through FilesHub's own AI routing, with a local fallback, and never contain links, email addresses or markup. A reader opens each note 3–180 minutes after it arrives, marks it read and flagged (Important on Gmail), moves it out of Spam if it landed there, and files it away. It touches only messages carrying a warm-up token it sent.

Warm-up notes count toward a mailbox's daily limit.

Measuring real engagement​

Once an hour FilesHub reads only the In-Reply-To and References headers of new inbox messages on marketing and outreach mailboxes, and marks the campaign send each reply answers (replied_at). Nothing in the inbox is flagged, moved or marked read. Bounce and complaint reports are read from the same inboxes.

The nightly tuner​

At 02:30 each mailbox is measured over the policy window (7 days by default), and the tuner acts on the result:

  • Repeated failures (3 sends in a row that the mailbox caused): a warm-up partner stops warming up; a marketing or outreach mailbox is paused from campaigns. Since 2026.10.21.1 a failure the recipient caused, such as an address that does not exist, a full mailbox or a reserved domain like example.com, neither counts nor breaks the run.

  • Trouble (hard bounces over 2%, complaints over 0.1%, or warm-up notes landing in spam): a marketing or outreach mailbox's limit is cut to 75% (never below its floor) and it is paused for 48 hours. Its warm-up volume goes up.

  • Healthy and busy (bounces under 1%, no complaints, 95%+ inbox, 80%+ of the limit used): the limit rises by 12%, at most once a week, up to the purpose's ceiling (marketing 150, outreach 40).

  • Ready for campaigns (since 2026.10.21.2): a warming marketing mailbox is approved for campaigns by itself once it has 14+ warm-up days, passing SPF/DKIM/DMARC, steady warm-up sends, inbox evidence and no failed send, and once its own warm-up notes reach recipients' inboxes at least 90% of the time. The owner is emailed; which product the mailbox sends for stays his choice.

Warm-up placement always means where a mailbox's own notes landed, which is its reputation.

Transactional mailboxes never have their limit cut or their sending paused. Only their warm-up volume moves. A rate with too little evidence behind it is null and never triggers anything. Every change, from the tuner, the API or Nova, is stored with its reason.

Since 2026.10.23.4 the server also runs the weekly review itself, once the newest review is seven days old. It reads the same numbers as GET /email-marketing/deliverability, takes one bounded policy step (the share of unconfirmed people per send is halved while a campaign mailbox bounces above 2%, and restored once every mailbox is under 1%), writes a summary and records the review. The owner is emailed only with what a person must do: failing DNS, a mailbox that cannot send, a notice that never arrived, the mailbox-check agent silent for a day, a project that will not resume by itself, or a ready mailbox no product uses. Fixes the system makes by itself are logged, never emailed.

API​

All endpoints are on the Management plane (https://fileshub.zaions.com/api/public/v1, Authorization: Bearer fh_pat_…). They need can_manage_email_marketing and a token for every project, because the mailboxes serve every product; otherwise they answer 403. An unknown id answers 404.

Method and pathWhat it does
GET /email-marketing/deliverabilityEvery mailbox: settings, warm-up state, persona, and last_7_days / last_30_days numbers. Also capacity, the policy with defaults and bounds, the last 30 tuning events, the last review and, since 2026.10.21.1, notices (the last 10 notices sent to the owner, with status and error). ?dns=1 adds SPF, DKIM and DMARC per domain.
PATCH /email-marketing/mailboxes/{id}Change daily_limit, warmup_enabled, warmup_max (1–100 or null), marketing_paused_until (within 30 days, or null) or is_active. reason is required and stored with each change. A marketing or outreach limit must sit inside the policy's floor and ceiling.
GET /email-marketing/deliverability/policyThe rules in force, the defaults and each rule's bounds.
PUT /email-marketing/deliverability/policy{"rules": {…}, "notes": "…"} — change some or all rules. One bad rule refuses the whole request (INVALID_RULES, each problem in error.details.rules). The version goes up by one.
POST /email-marketing/deliverability/reviews{"summary": "…", "model": "…", "changes_applied": […]} records a review → 201 with next_review_due.
GET /email-marketing/deliverability/reviewsThe latest reviews, newest first: id, at, summary, changes_applied, model, reviewed_by. ?limit= 1–50 (default 10). Since 2026.10.23.4.
POST /email-marketing/deliverability/reviews/runRuns the server's own weekly review now. {"dry_run": true} answers what it would find and change (200) and saves and sends nothing; otherwise 201 with review_id, summary, model, changes, owner_actions and notified. Since 2026.10.23.4.
GET /email-marketing/warmup/personasPersonas, relationships (kind, chapter, story so far), and the available kinds and life arcs.
PATCH /email-marketing/warmup/personas/{id}display_name, bio, voice, interests (up to 5), timezone. Plain text only.
PATCH /email-marketing/warmup/relationships/{id}kind, chapter, story_summary. Plain text only.

The per-mailbox numbers come straight from the send and warm-up records:

  • campaign: sent, failed, bounced, complained, opened, clicked, replied, unsubscribed, and bounce_rate, complaint_rate, reply_rate, click_rate (null below min_sends_for_rates sends).
  • warmup: sent, received, found_inbox, found_spam, rescued, not_found, replied, inbox_share (null below min_found_for_inbox_share).
  • utilisation (sends a day ÷ limit), failure_streak and last_failure.

The older GET /email-marketing/warmup still answers. Since this release its ramp is the per-purpose map shown above, and its rows include every mailbox in warm-up.

In Nova​

Dashboards → Mailbox overview (since 2026.10.21.1) lists every mailbox with its purpose, status, daily limit, sent today, and sends in the last 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 60, 90, 180 and 360 days. Filter by Purpose, and choose What to count: all real mail, marketing mail, other mail or warm-up notes. Open a mailbox for its Sending history & health panel, which gives the status and its reason in plain words, the week's and month's numbers, sends per window, the last changes and its persona.

What to expect​

Warm-up replies come from your own network. They help inbox placement but are not engagement. Real recipients decide your reputation: keep hard bounces under 2% and complaints well under 0.3%, the ceiling Gmail and Yahoo enforce for bulk senders, and expect cold outreach to draw a few percent of replies. A healthy mailbox carries roughly 50–150 cold emails a day after four to six weeks of warm-up, so a daily volume in the thousands needs mailboxes spread over several domains.
